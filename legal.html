1. What we collect

  • If you create an account: your email address, chosen username, and a securely hashed (not plain-text) password.
  • Your chat messages, saved so your conversation history is available when you log back in.
  • If you use Guest Mode: nothing is stored — no messages, no identifying data. Only an anonymous total count of guest sessions is kept.

2. Why we collect it

Solely to operate the service: verifying your email at signup, letting you log in, and restoring your chat history. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

3. Your consent

By checking the consent box at signup, you agree to this policy and to our storing the data described above. You can withdraw consent at any time by asking us to delete your account and data (see Grievance contact below).

4. Security

Passwords are hashed and salted before storage — we never store or display passwords in readable form. Given this app's architecture, we are transparent that data protection has practical limits; see our security notes for developers.

5. Content rules

You may not use this service to request or generate: obscene or sexually explicit material, child sexual abuse material, instructions for weapons or explosives, terrorism-related planning, or detailed self-harm methods. Our automated system enforces this in two tiers:

  • Instant permanent ban, no warning: requests involving child sexual abuse material, or instructions for making bombs/explosives. These are treated as zero-tolerance and result in immediate, permanent account suspension.
  • Warning, then ban: other flagged violations (e.g. general obscene content, other self-harm or violence-related requests) result in a first warning; a repeat violation results in permanent suspension.

Suspended accounts lose access to the service. If you believe your account was suspended in error, contact the Grievance Officer below.
